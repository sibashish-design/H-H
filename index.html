05 / Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Where is H&H Concepts furniture manufactured? +

All our furniture is engineered and crafted at our 50,000 sq ft manufacturing facility in Mohabbewala Industrial Area, Dehradun, Uttarakhand, supported by imported BIFMA-certified components.

What warranty is provided with H&H Concepts furniture? +

We provide a 24-Month On-Site Warranty on all executive chairs, ergo office series, and sofa collections, and a 36-Month Warranty on hotel room bed suites and motorized recliners.

How do I get B2B or trade pricing for bulk orders? +

Select any item on our website or visit The Shop and click "Send Direct Enquiry". Our commercial sales team will send you a custom project quote within 2 hours.

Do you offer custom upholstery, sizes & finish options? +

Yes! We offer extensive custom fabric, genuine European leather, wood finish stains (Walnut, Oak, Mahogany), and frame powder-coating for commercial fitouts.

What is the delivery timeline for executive and hotel fitouts? +

Standard catalog items ship within 3–7 business days across India. Custom hotel fitouts and bulk contract orders are delivered within 14–21 business days with white-glove installation.
