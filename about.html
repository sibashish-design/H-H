Our Origins

Built in Dehradun,
Driven by Excellence

Founded under the umbrella of FBI Industries, H&H Concepts represents over two decades of technical mastery in ergonomic seating, executive desks, lounge furniture, and hospitality fitouts.

Our 50,000 sq ft manufacturing plant in Mohabbewala Industrial Area, Dehradun, houses state-of-the-art metal fabrication, precision woodcraft, and upholstery studios. Coupled with our Experience Studio at Crown Tower, Ballupur, we offer an uncompromised hands-on experience to trade professionals and clients.
